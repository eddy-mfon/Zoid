# ZOID Storefront Design Direction

## Three Approaches

## Selected Approach: Concrete Ritual

### Design Movement
Contemporary editorial brutalism fused with football archive culture and Lagos street-poster composition.

### Core Principles
1. Make the product feel curated, not merely listed: every jersey carries a point of view.
2. Use contrast as a narrative device: black for conviction, white for clarity, pigment pink for the pulse of ambition.
3. Prefer asymmetry, oversized type, and cropped imagery over predictable centered ecommerce layouts.
4. Let motion behave like sport: directional, snappy, and purposeful rather than decorative.

### Color Philosophy
Black is the ground of grit and focus. White opens breathing room and makes the editorial voice unmistakable. Pigment pink is the ownable emotional current: a vivid signal for desire, progress, and the moment a player decides to keep going. Use it sparingly but decisively so it reads as brand energy, not decoration.

### Layout Paradigm
A full-bleed hero with offset editorial copy, horizontal product rails that break the container, and alternating story bands that pull the eye diagonally down the page. The grid is a quiet structure beneath expressive overlaps, cropping, and oversized numerals.

### Signature Elements
- Thin pink route-lines and corner marks that echo tactical diagrams.
- Giant condensed headlines with occasional pink word swaps.
- Product cards that tilt, reveal metadata, and use clipped image framing rather than uniform rounded boxes.

### Interaction Philosophy
Every interaction should feel like making a selection on the pitch: direct, responsive, and legible. Hover states reveal context, drags move the product rail, and cart actions confirm with a quick pink pulse. Keyboard focus remains visible and intentional.

### Animation
Hero copy enters on staggered vertical clips; product rails use drag-friendly horizontal movement with snap points; cards lift by a few pixels and rotate slightly on hover; story numbers drift at low amplitude on scroll; all non-essential motion is disabled or simplified for reduced-motion users. UI transitions stay within 180–280ms and use a firm ease-out.

### Typography System
Use Anton for large display headlines and Archivo for navigation, metadata, body copy, and product details. Headlines are uppercase, tightly tracked, and often split across lines. Body copy is compact, calm, and never wider than 42rem.

### Brand Essence
ZOID is an international African sportbrand curating the jerseys, stories, and symbols that help people rise beyond circumstance. Personality: **gritty, discerning, expansive**.

### Brand Voice
Headlines are declarative and kinetic. CTAs are invitations into a point of view, not generic conversion language. Microcopy is concise, observant, and human.

Example lines:
- “THE HEIGHT IS YOURS TO DECIDE.”
- “CURATED FOR THE ONES STILL RISING.”

### Wordmark & Logo
A custom compressed Z mark built from two opposing diagonal cuts, like a fast-forward symbol interrupted by a route line. Pair it with a compact ZOID wordmark in a condensed grotesk, with the pigment-pink dot as the signature punctuation.

### Signature Brand Color
Pigment Pink — `#E7194B` — a saturated red-pink that carries urgency, pride, and forward motion.

## Style Decisions
- Use a dark, cinematic hero with light text and a pink signal color.
- Avoid generic centered layouts, purple gradients, and uniform rounded cards.
- Treat product imagery as editorial evidence and archive material, not generic stock photography.
- Keep commerce practical: visible price, quick add, accessible controls, and a cart drawer-style interaction on the page.

## Style Decisions

- Product thumbnails are treated as archive evidence through crop, contrast, field labels, and material-first presentation.
- Pigment-pink tactical route lines, diagonal cut marks, stamped numerals, and field-note metadata appear across major sections.
- Commerce copy stays functional, while section headlines remain declarative and culturally specific.
- The header wordmark carries a compressed, marked-up studio identity rather than reading as neutral navigation text.
